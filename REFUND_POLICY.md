## Summary

- ✅ Refunds available for **unused credits only**
- ✅ Request within **14 days** of purchase

- ✅ Manual refund process via support contact
- ✅ Processed through Stripe (same payment method)

## 1. Overview

At Sanyla, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. This Refund Policy explains when and how you can request a refund for credit pack purchases made via Stripe.

**All credit purchases are processed through Stripe Checkout.**

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligible for Refund

You may request a refund if:

✅ **Unused Credits**: You have NOT used the credits you purchased  
✅ **Within 14 Days**: Request is made within 14 calendar days of purchase  
✅ **Proof of Purchase**: You can provide the transaction ID or email receipt  
✅ **Technical Issue**: Service malfunction prevented you from using credits (evaluated case-by-case)

### 2.2 NOT Eligible for Refund

You **cannot** receive a refund if:

❌ **Credits Already Used**: You generated campaigns, rewrites, or consumed credits  
❌ **Past 14 Days**: More than 14 days have passed since purchase  
❌ **Free Credits**: Refund applies only to purchased credits (not free signup credits)  
❌ **Partial Use**: You used some credits and want a refund for the remainder  
❌ **Dissatisfaction with Content**: AI-generated content quality does not meet expectations  
❌ **Changed Mind**: General change of mind after using the Service

## 4. How to Request a Refund

### Step 1: Contact Support

Submit a refund request via our website contact form at:

**https://www.sanyla.site**

### Step 2: Provide Information

Include the following in your request:

- **Email Address**: The email associated with your Sanyla account
- **Transaction ID**: Found in your Stripe email receipt (starts with `ch_` or `pi_`)
- **Purchase Date**: Date of the credit purchase
- **Reason for Refund**: Brief explanation (optional but helpful)
- **Credit Pack**: Which pack you purchased (Starter, Pro, or Power)

### Step 3: Verification

We will verify:

- Your account and purchase history
- That credits have NOT been used
- That the request is within 14 days of purchase
- Payment status in Stripe

### Step 4: Refund Decision

- **Approval**: If eligible, refund processed within 5-7 business days
- **Denial**: If ineligible, we will explain why and offer alternatives (if applicable)

---

## 5. Refund Processing

### 5.1 Method

- Refunds are processed through **Stripe** to the **original payment method**
- You will receive the refund to the same card or account used for purchase
- Stripe handles all refund transactions securely

### 5.2 Timeline

- **Approval**: Refund initiated within 1-2 business days
- **Processing**: Stripe processes refunds within 5-7 business days
- **Bank Posting**: Your bank may take an additional 3-10 business days to post the refund

**Total Time**: Expect up to 10-14 business days from approval to see the refund in your account.

### 5.3 Currency

- Refunds issued in **Euros (EUR)**
- Currency conversion rates (if applicable) are determined by your card issuer

---

## 6. No Automatic Refunds

**Refunds are NOT automatic.**

- You must manually request a refund via our support contact
- We do not automatically refund unused credits
- Accounts closed with unused credits forfeit those credits (unless a refund is requested before closure)

---

## 7. Partial Refunds

**We do NOT offer partial refunds.**

- If you used even 1 credit, the entire purchase is non-refundable
- Credit packs are sold as a complete package
- Example: If you purchased 500 credits (Pro pack) and used 50 credits, you cannot receive a refund for the remaining 450 credits

---

## 8. Exceptions and Special Cases

### 8.1 Technical Failures

If a technical issue on our end prevented you from using purchased credits:

- Contact us with details of the issue
- We will investigate and may offer:
  - Full refund (if credits were never accessible)
  - Partial credit compensation (if credits were partially usable)
  - Extension of credit validity (if applicable)

### 8.2 Fraudulent Purchases

If you suspect fraudulent activity on your account:

- Contact us immediately
- Also contact your card issuer and Stripe support
- We will cooperate with investigations
- Refunds for fraud are handled case-by-case

### 8.3 Duplicate Charges

If you were accidentally charged twice for the same purchase:

- Contact us with both transaction IDs
- We will issue a full refund for the duplicate charge immediately

---

## 9. What Counts as "Used Credits"

Credits are considered **used** if you have:

- Generated a campaign (full or partial)
- Requested an AI post rewrite
- Consumed credits through any Service feature

Credits are **NOT considered used** if you:

- Only logged into your account
- Browsed the dashboard
- Connected social accounts
- Scheduled but did NOT generate content

---

## 10. Refund vs. Account Deletion

**Refund Request:**
- You can request a refund and keep your account active
- If approved, credits are removed from your account
- You can purchase credits again in the future

**Account Deletion:**
- Deleting your account does NOT automatically trigger a refund
- If you want a refund, request it BEFORE deleting your account
- After deletion, refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis

## 11. Disputes and Chargebacks

### 11.1 Contact Us First

**Before filing a chargeback with your bank**, please contact us to resolve the issue.

- Chargebacks can take 60-90 days to resolve
- We can often resolve issues faster through direct contact
- Chargebacks may result in account suspension pending investigation

### 11.2 Chargeback Consequences

If you file a chargeback:

- We will provide evidence to your card issuer (purchase records, usage logs)
- Your account may be suspended until the dispute is resolved
- If the chargeback is found to be fraudulent, your account may be permanently banned

### 11.3 Dispute Resolution

For refund disputes:

1. **First**: Contact us via the website to resolve informally
2. **Second**: If unresolved, escalate via email with details
3. **Third**: As a last resort, contact your card issuer or Stripe support

We aim to resolve all disputes fairly and transparently.
